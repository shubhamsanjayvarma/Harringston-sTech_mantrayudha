# 01. NovaMart Hackathon Wedge & 2-Minute Demo Script

## 1. Product Overview & Wedge
- **Product**: NovaMart - Ultra-fast & Curated Everyday Essentials
- **Core Wedge**: Instant hyper-local fulfillment (10-15 minute delivery) bridging fresh grocery staples and premium smart lifestyle appliances in one unified, friction-free interface.
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Pristine white and warm natural surfaces, restrained dark slate typography, vibrant natural green brand accents, and high-density, touch-friendly product tiles.

---

## 2. The 2-Minute Judge Walkthrough Script

| Timestamp | Screen State / User Action | Narrative & Technical Hook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:25** | Initial load at 1440px desktop viewport. Notice announcement bar, header with "NOVA MART", Indiranagar delivery badge, and hero section. | *"Welcome to NovaMart. Our interface is designed for instantaneous local discovery. From the crisp announcement bar down to our curated hero and benefit row, every element gives confidence and immediacy."* |
